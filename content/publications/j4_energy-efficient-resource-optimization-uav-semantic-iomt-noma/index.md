abstract: "Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-assisted Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) networks have emerged as a flexible solution for providing connectivity in remote and emergency healthcare scenarios. However, limited onboard energy, constrained communication and computational resources, and dynamic user distributions pose significant challenges to reliable and efficient medical data delivery. This paper develops an energy-efficient resource optimization framework for UAV-assisted IoMT networks by integrating semantic-aware communications with non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA). By combining semantic-aware transmission, which reduces redundant data delivery through task-oriented information extraction, with NOMA-based spectrum sharing, the proposed framework significantly improves both spectral efficiency and energy utilization in dense medical networks. The problem is formulated as a joint energy efficiency maximization task that simultaneously optimizes UAV deployment, user association, power allocation, and computational resource assignment under semantic reconstruction constraints. Due to the mixed-integer and non-convex nature of the problem, a hierarchical low-complexity solution is proposed. Specifically, fast global K-means clustering with fuzzy refinement is used for UAV placement and user association, followed by a grouping-matching mechanism for NOMA subcarrier allocation and closed-form resource optimization. The proposed framework ensures convergence with reduced computational complexity while preserving semantic consistency in medical report reconstruction. Simulation results demonstrate that integrating semantic-aware transmission with NOMA improves overall energy efficiency and resource utilization compared to conventional schemes. However, the current framework relies on a predefined semantic similarity threshold and assumes a fixed UAV altitude to maintain tractability. Extending the design toward adaptive and learning-driven semantic-aware optimization and jointly optimizing additional system parameters, represents an important direction for future work."
